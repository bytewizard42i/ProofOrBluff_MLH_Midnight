# 1. Business Overview

## The Product

**Proof or Bluff (POB)** is a privacy-native card game DApp built on the Midnight Network. Players face an Ai opponent in a digital version of the classic bluffing game "Bullshit" / "Cheat", where zero-knowledge proofs resolve disputes without exposing private game state.

## The Business

POB operates as a **hybrid free-to-play (F2P) game** with multiple revenue streams:

1. **Ad-supported free tier** — Crypto product advertising (premium CPMs)
2. **Ad-free premium tier** — One-time purchase or subscription
3. **Digital asset sales** — Card skins, Ai personas, table themes
4. **Casino Mode wagering** — Rake/fees on real-money games (realDeal)
5. **Asset management platform licensing** — B2B SaaS for other game developers

## Mission Statement

> Build the first game where blockchain privacy isn't a feature — it's the fundamental reason the game works. Then use that game to prove the commercial viability of privacy-preserving gaming infrastructure.

### Ad-Free Purchase & Subscription Deep-Dive

#### Why Players Pay to Remove Ads

The psychology is straightforward: once a player is emotionally invested in the Ai relationship, ads break immersion. The Charming Liar is mid-taunt, and a Kraken exchange ad interrupts — that's the moment the player thinks *"I'd pay to stop this."*

Industry data (2024-2025):
- **74% of U.S. mobile gamers** tolerate rewarded video ads for in-game rewards
- **3.5% of all players** make any in-app purchase (AppsFlyer)
- **Ad removal IAP conversion**: 5–10% of players who see ads regularly (higher than average IAP because the value proposition is crystal clear)
- **Subscription conversion**: 12–18% of engaged players for battle-pass-style offerings
- **Average time to first purchase**: 3–7 days of active play
- **86% of developers** who added ads to IAP games saw no decrease in IAP revenue (Facebook Audience Network study)

#### POB Ad-Free Tiers — Detailed Analysis

**Tier A: One-Time Ad Removal — $4.99**

| Metric | Value | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Target audience | Players who value clean experience | "I just want to play without interruption" |
| Expected conversion | 5–8% of total users | Crypto audiences have higher willingness to pay |
| LTV | $4.99 (lifetime) | One-time purchase |
| Break-even vs ads | ~32 days | $4.99 ÷ $0.155/day ad revenue = 32 days |
| Strategic value | High | Converts "annoyed free user" into "invested user" who buys cosmetics later |

**Price anchoring**: $4.99 is below the psychological $5 threshold. Players see: "Less than a coffee to never see ads again." This is the highest-converting price point for ad removal in casual games.

**Tier B: Monthly Subscription — $1.99/mo**

| Metric | Value | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Target audience | Power players, variety seekers | "I play every day and want the full experience" |
| Expected conversion | 3–5% of total users | Recurring commitment, needs ongoing value |
| Average retention | 4–6 months | Industry average for game subscriptions |
| LTV | $7.96–$11.94 | 4-6 months × $1.99 |
| Perks included | Ad-free + exclusive Ai personas + priority matchmaking + monthly cosmetic drop | Must justify recurring cost |
| Break-even vs one-time | Month 3 | Subscriber revenue exceeds $4.99 one-time at month 3 |

**Key insight**: The subscription isn't just "no ads" — it's the **premium Ai experience**. Subscribers get exclusive Ai personalities that free players can't access. This makes the subscription feel like unlocking new characters in a fighting game, not just removing annoyances.

**Tier C: Season Pass — $9.99/season (3 months)**

| Metric | Value | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Target audience | Engaged, competitive, collectors | "I want to earn exclusive stuff" |
| Expected conversion | 8–12% of premium users (subscribers/purchasers) | Battle pass psychology |
| Includes | Ad-free + exclusive card skins track (10 tiers) + seasonal Ai persona + cosmetic rewards + ranked leaderboard access | Progressive unlock creates daily login incentive |
| LTV per season | $9.99 | Renewable each season |
| Renewal rate | 60–70% | Industry average for season passes |

**The battle pass model works because**: Each daily login earns progress toward the next cosmetic unlock. Players feel compelled to "get their money's worth" — driving retention. The free track gets 4 items; the paid track gets 10. Free players see what they're missing.

#### The "Conversion Funnel" — How a Free Player Becomes a Paying Player

```
Day 1:   Download → play 2 games → see 4-6 ads → "Fun game, ads are fine"
Day 3:   Playing daily → 15+ ads seen → starting to feel the friction
Day 5:   Mid-taunt ad interruption → "Ugh, that broke the moment"
         └── TRIGGER: In-game prompt: "Remove ads forever for $4.99"
Day 7:   Buys ad removal ($4.99) → immediately happier experience
Day 14:  Sees subscriber-exclusive Ai persona "Midnight Phantom" → curious
Day 21:  Upgrades to $1.99/mo subscription for exclusive personas
Day 30:  Season 1 launches → buys Season Pass ($9.99) for exclusive card skins
Day 90:  Renews season pass → LTV now $16.97 and climbing
```

**Total LTV of a converted user**: $4.99 (ad removal) + $1.99 × 6 months (subscription) + $9.99 × 2 seasons (pass) = **$36.95 in year 1**. Compare to ad-only LTV of ~$0.20/day × 365 = $73/year — but only 70% of users stay free. The blended model captures value from *both* populations.

# 5. Scaling Plan

## Technical Scaling

### Infrastructure Tiers

| Users | Infrastructure | Monthly Cost Estimate |
|-------|---------------|---------------------|
| 0–10K | Single server, shared proof server | $200–$500 |
| 10K–100K | Load-balanced servers, dedicated proof servers | $2K–$10K |
| 100K–1M | Multi-region deployment, CDN, clustered proof servers | $15K–$50K |
| 1M+ | Global edge network, distributed proof infrastructure | $50K–$200K |

### Proof Server Scaling

ZK proof generation is the computational bottleneck. Scaling strategy:

1. **Phase 1**: Players run local proof servers (Docker) — zero server cost
2. **Phase 2**: Hosted proof server pool for mobile/web players
3. **Phase 3**: Proof generation marketplace — third parties compete to generate proofs
4. **Phase 4**: Hardware-accelerated proof generation (FPGA/ASIC as they become available)

### Ai Agent Scaling

| Scale | Ai Approach | Cost per Session |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Iteration 1–2** | Scripted responses (zero Ai cost) | $0.00 |
| **Iteration 3 (BYOK)** | Player's own API key | $0.00 (player pays) |
| **Iteration 3 (hosted)** | Subsidized Ai with ad revenue offset | $0.01–$0.05 |
| **Iteration 3 (premium)** | Full Ai, included in subscription | Bundled |
